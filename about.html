My Full Story

Twelve years in the higher education sector with experience working in government, universities, education agencies and as an international student myself.

01

Arrived in Sydney as an international student

Landed in Sydney, Australia in 2016 as an international student working with Australian clients from a distance.

02

Worked inside the Australian Higher Education sector

At Macquarie University, I ran compliance checks on new applicants and helped new students settle in. At the Australian National University, I wore multiple hats, working with faculties, marketing and recruitment teams, and in my last role, ran the international student recruitment function.

03

Led teams in professional Australian settings

Running a recruitment function meant working with students and institutions from all over the world. The one thing that stuck: there's no single right answer, and no two situations are the same.

04

Public service in the New South Wales Government

I managed the international engagement and partnerships for the NSW Government's trade and investment arm, connecting universities and schools with new commercial opportunities, internationally. I also ran the programs supporting international students in the state.

05

Now based in UAE supporting families and institutions

After a decade in Australia, I wanted a new challenge, and Australian citizenship meant I could go looking for one. I've sat in boardrooms with state ministers and vice-chancellors, and advised newly appointed ambassadors and consul-generals on the state's education priorities. At the same time, I've sat with a nervous student figuring out their first move.
