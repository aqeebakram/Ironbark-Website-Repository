01 — Premise

Most familiesarrive late.

By the time most families start looking seriously, several doors have already closed quietly. Nothing went wrong. Nobody told them the decisions had already been made.

i

The choices that matter come years before the application

Subject selection in Year 10 and 11 decides which Australian degrees stay reachable. That closure happens silently, and it is rarely explained at the time.

ii

Fit is a research problem, not a ranking exercise

The right course, city and university for one student is rarely the highest-ranked. It depends on the child, the budget, and what happens after graduation.

iii

Cost is settled long before the deposit

Total cost to graduation can be modelled years out. Most families see the real number only once they are committed and it is too late to change course.

05 — Case note · composite, illustrative

She had the grades for the ranking. She did not have the prerequisites for the course.

A Dubai family came to me in Year 12 with a shortlist of highly-ranked universities and a daughter set on veterinary science. The subject combination chosen two years earlier had quietly closed the prerequisite. Nobody had raised it.

We remapped to a related pathway with an articulated entry route, modelled total cost to graduation against the original plan, and found a campus with a stronger placement record and a lower fee base. She started the following February.
