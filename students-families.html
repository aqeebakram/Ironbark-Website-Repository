The Process

Five stages. The first one costs nothing, and there is no pressure to continue past it.

01

Discovery Call — FREE

A free, 45-minute conversation to understand your aspirations and goals. This is where we get to know each other, with no obligation.

02

Situation Mapping

A structured assessment through a series of questions, mapping constraints, education policy, migration policy, budget ceiling, academic profile, and career goals, before any recommendation is made.

03

Pathway Recommendation

A specific, justified recommendation, personalised and tied directly to your own situation, not a list of options to sort through yourself.

04

Total Cost & Return Modelling

A toolkit that gives clarity on the investment you will make over the study period and beyond. It is carefully crafted to ensure there are no surprises later.

05

Execution Support

Applications, scholarships, offer negotiation, visa guidance, accommodation, and real guidance on navigating the first few months in a new country.
